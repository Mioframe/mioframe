## Preserved scenarios and failure paths

- **RepoExplorerPane "Add" action**: entry point (floating action anchored to the bottom-right of the repository-explorer pane via `FabContainer`), interaction tier (primary floating action), accessible name (`"Add"`), and behavior (click sets `showEntryAddSheet = true`, opening `EntryAddSheet`) are all unchanged. The visibility guard `v-if="canEditDirectoryContents !== false"` is untouched: the action is hidden only when the widget explicitly reports non-editable contents, and remains visible when editable or when editability is not yet known (`undefined`) — proven by the three existing `RepoExplorerPane.test.ts` cases covering `true`/`false`/`undefined`, unchanged.
- **`FabContainer`**: placement, auto-hide-on-scroll, and overlay/teleport ownership are unchanged; it remains outside Material ownership and was not edited.
- No disabled state existed for this action before or after migration; the product still omits the entire `FabContainer` rather than rendering a disabled action, matching both the legacy and canonical component's lack of a disabled affordance.
- No loading/tooltip capability was in use before migration, so none is lost.
